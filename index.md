## Overview

This workshop provides a hands-on introduction to **computational analysis of untargeted metabolomics data**, with a focus on widely used community tools:

* [GNPS](https://gnps.ucsd.edu/) & [GNPS2](https://gnps2.org/)
* [mzmine](https://mzio.io/mzmine-news/)
* [SIRIUS](https://bio.informatik.uni-jena.de/software/sirius/)

### Learning Goals

By the end of the workshop, participants will be able to:

* Perform spectrum annotation and molecular networking.
* Process LC-MS/MS data using mzine and SIRIUS.
* Explore repository-scale metabolomics data.

### Expected Background

* Basic familiarity with mass spectrometry-based metabolomics.
* No advanced programming experience required.
